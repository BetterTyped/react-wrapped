         engine 24.1-31.1: the stage fills the frame, plays, the camera pulls back to show it is a frame
         share 31-37.2: five story-format videos on a row travelling at a constant speed, cuts left to right
         finale 37.1-45.1: claim grey -> ink, mark drawn, name wiped in, install typed, link, hold 2.4 s
BANNED:  a dark void with small things in it (every shot fills 60%+ of the frame), glows and particles,
         three cards in a row, KPI grids, titles that move after they land, the docked logo, any invented UI
QA:      0 s (thumbnail is never empty), 7.6 s (bars landing), 10.5 s (lockup), 15.4 s (code + clock),
