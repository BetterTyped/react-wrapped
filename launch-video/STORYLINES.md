# Storylines

## A. From a table to a year (built)

Device: life before and after. Form: object-to-object chain. A product's users had a whole year, and the product shows
them a report. The report's numbers stand up as the library's bar chart, the name lands, and from there each shot hands
over to the next: the code that makes a chart, every chart there is, a real video playing, every user getting their
own, the install line. Picked because a product builder recognises the table: it is what they ship today.

## B. One number, all the way down

Device: a number lands. Form: an endless zoom. "1,687" counts up full frame, the camera dives into the comma and finds
the week it came from, dives into the busiest day and finds the hour, then pulls all the way out to a share card on a
phone. Strong picture, weaker reason for a product builder to care.

## C. Rebuilt live

Device: the feature as the hook. Form: one frame that rebuilds. A single stage never leaves the frame while its props
are edited on the side: palette, format from 16:9 to 9:16, transition, chart type. The most honest demo, but it reads as
a configurator, and the film had to sell the outcome.
